Whale-girl (DeepSeek) walk row — 4 frames. Her apron hides her legs, so the row
reads as standing. This redraw is about showing the legs.

LAYOUT: one row, 4 frames, image 2048x512 (4 cells of 512x512). Background flat
MAGENTA #FF00FF, one colour, no gradient, no vignette. No grid, text, watermark
or ground shadow. Character ~350x460 px per cell, same scale in all four cells,
feet at 92% of cell height. Faces the viewer, turned slightly to her right.

CHARACTER (same girl, do not redesign): chibi whale-girl, short deep-blue hair
fading to white tips, two whale-fluke hair ornaments, white frilled headdress,
navy-blue maid dress with white lace trim, a small whale-tail ornament at the
waist, one arm holding a small round whale plush, whale tail behind her, white
thigh-high socks, dark rounded shoes. Head-to-body 1:2. 1px navy outline,
2-3 tones per material, ~32 colours.

LEGS — the whole point:
 The apron and skirt hem end AT THE KNEE, not below it. Both legs from knee to
   shoe are fully visible in every frame, at least 12 px wide each.
 Both shoes fully visible, at least 26 px wide, flat horizontal soles.
 Two separate legs, never merged into one column.

WALK CYCLE:
 frame 1 CONTACT: legs spread wide, both soles on the ground, front foot
   forward — the widest stance of the cycle.
 frame 2 PASS: rear leg swings through, knee lifted 20-30 px, that foot off the
   ground and clearly higher than the other.
 frame 3 CONTACT: mirror of frame 1, the other leg forward now.
 frame 4 PASS: mirror of frame 2, the other knee lifted.
 Feet alternate: if the left foot leads in frame 1, the right foot leads in
 frame 3. The free arm swings opposite the leading leg.

STYLE: 16-bit RPG pixel art, hard pixel edges, no anti-aliasing on the
silhouette, muted palette matching her sheet.
