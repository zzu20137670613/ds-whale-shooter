# 块 2 · 星海（右上 NE）· v2

> v1 判了：**画成一艘浮在星空里的船**（3/4 等角，带一圈栏杆）。
>
> 根因跟第 1 块的房间坑**是同一个**：我写的是 ``an open observation deck floating in a star sea``——
> **"deck"是一个"东西"，模型就会把它当物体从外面看**。凡是能被整体观看的物体，一定给你 3/4。
>
> 过关的公式在第 1 块那儿已经验证过：**主语必须是"一块表面"**。
> v2 改成 ``This image IS A FLOOR: pale plating and dark metal seams, seen straight down. Not a ship, not a platform, not an object - a surface.``
>
> 另外把三个"立着"的诱因全拆了：
> - **栏杆** → 加进 Negative：``railing, balustrade, handrail, guardrail``（v1 那圈栏杆就是"船"的元凶）
> - **罗盘玫瑰/鲸尾** → 改成 ``inlaid flush``（平嵌），不许浮雕
> - **星空在四周** → 改成 **"透过地板的缝隙看到下面的星海"**（``Stars come through the floor, not around it``），这样星星是地板的一部分，不是平台周围的背景

```
2D top-down game map asset seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE (90 degrees, orthographic plan view). This image IS A FLOOR: pale plating and dark metal seams, seen straight down. Not a ship, not a platform, not an object - a surface. GAPS: the plating is torn away in a few places and through the gaps you see the star field far below. Stars come through the floor, not around it.

OCCUPANCY: >=60% clean walkable plating; the gaps and starfield occupy the rest; asymmetric.

LIGHT: even and cold, no sun, no god rays; the plating is lit by its own pale reflection, the stars are the only sparkle.

Palette: plating #B9C4D2, plate shadow #6E7A8C, star white #EAF2FF, void navy #0E1A2E, inlay gold #B08A3C, whale blue #2E5B8A.

Details, ALL flush with the floor, none standing: long inlaid seams between plates; a brass compass rose inlaid flush; two or three round glass panels set flush in the floor, stars showing through them; small flat bolts and access hatches; a whale-tail motif inlaid flush in gold near one side; a few flat star-mote sparks on the surface. CENTRE: a wide clean circular inlay of pale plating, 14% of frame width, EMPTY - no figure, no statue, no console, no furniture.

Size: 3:2 or 16:9, full bleed, composed for a 16:9 centre crop (landmarks inside the central 84%).
```
