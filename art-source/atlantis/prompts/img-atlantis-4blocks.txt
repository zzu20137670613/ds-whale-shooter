# S3 亚特兰蒂斯 · 四块底图提示词

> 用法同 S2：一次一张 2752×1536，我切 4 张 → 你高清 2× → 我拼成 5504×3072，
> 四块合成 4×4 屏。**CAMERA / STYLE / SEAM 三节逐字保留。**
>
> 一条重要取舍：第七世写的「潮涨一层，城露一间」是**运行时机制**，
> 一张图配潮位遮罩/换皮才能做到，**不是靠多画几张图**。所以这里每块画的是
> **"退潮露出、涨潮会淹"的那种半淹状态**——水线、湿痕、露出半截的门都在图里，
> 潮位由我在代码里做（遮罩 + 通行区切换）。先按静态可玩做，潮汐后加。

## 布局（四块 2×2，流程 SW → NW → NE → SE，越走越深）

| 块 | 位置 | 内容 | 深度 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | 左下 SW | 海门 + 折断沉船（深海之忆外） | 最暗 |
| B | 左上 NW | 深海之忆：半开青石门 + 水影浮名 | 更暗，只有门缝一线蓝 |
| C | 右上 NE | 摄政之谏：穹顶裂缝天光 + 镜面水 + 黑鲸石像 | 冷银，最静 |
| D | 右下 SE | 加冕：潮退露阶 + 石座 | 暖色收尾，冷银转浅金 |

---

## CAMERA（逐字保留）

True orthographic **plan view**, straight down. No horizon, no water surface
seen edge-on, no visible walls in elevation, no vanishing point. Water is read
as **a flat sheet with what lies beneath it faintly visible** — never as a
vertical wall of water. A light shaft from a ceiling crack is read as **a hard
wedge of light lying on the floor**, not as a beam standing up. Stone steps are
read as **treads from above**.

## STYLE（逐字保留）

Anime-inspired hand-painted 2D top-down game art, soft cel shading, clean 1–2 px
line work on landmarks, matte finish, wide flat colour areas so characters and
props stay readable. Wet surfaces get a soft even sheen, not mirror-gloss.

## SEAM（逐字保留）

This image is one of four panels. Keep the **outer 120 px of every edge free of
unique landmarks, text, and hard shadow edges** — continuous ground or water
texture only, and let structures that must continue (floor slabs, water line,
stairs) run straight off the edge perpendicular to it. No border, frame,
vignette, or corner darkening.

---

## A · 海门 + 沉船（左下 SW）

The drowned outskirts. Fine pale seabed sand in soft ripples, drifting silt,
a scatter of broken coral and rubble. At the top-left of the panel a **broken
shipwreck** lies on its side, half sunk into sand: ribbed hull timbers, a
snapped mast, tattered sail fabric laid flat by water. To the right, the first
**stone gate architecture** appears — a massive square doorway of pale cyan
stone, **half-open**, one door slab pushed inward at an angle, the gap reading
as pure deep blue. Barnacle crust and a tide mark ring the gate's base.
Palette: seabed sand #C9BFA8, silt #8E8B7E, cyan stone #6F9C9C, deep blue
#10222E (in the doorway only), rust #6E5A38.

## B · 深海之忆（左上 NW）

Inside the gate, the darkest panel of the map. The seabed continues, but a
**thin sheet of still water** now covers the floor, so stone slabs and rubble
show through it, slightly blue-shifted. At the centre a **second stone door
stands half-open**, and from its gap a **single narrow blade of cold blue light**
falls across the floor — the brightest thing here, and the only strong light.
Faint **crossed-out name marks** float on the water surface as pale scratches
(abstract glyph strokes, **not legible characters**), arranged in loose rows
like a drowned list. Two broken sail-ribs and a coil of rotted rope lie near
the door (collectables). Palette: #0C1A24 ground, wet stone #2E4B52, water sheen
#3E6B6E, the light blade #9FD8E8, silt #5C5A4E.

## C · 摄政之谏（右上 NE）

A drowned hall, cold and still. Above, a **crack in the dome** — in plan view
an irregular jagged opening — lets a **wedge of pale sky light** lie across the
floor at an angle. The floor is **thin water like a mirror**: the hall's
pillars and the throne are reflected in it, soft and dim. At the hall's centre
an **empty throne embedded in black reef** — pale stone seat sunk in dark
branching coral. Around it, **whale-shaped stone statues** stand in a ring,
seen from above as long tapering slabs with whale flukes, **all oriented toward
the throne**. Three small rings of carved glyph marks mark three question
points on the floor (abstract marks, no real characters). Palette: reef black
#16222A, cold silver #C9D6DC, wet stone #7E8C93, sky light #EAF4F2, faint gold
#6E5A38.

## D · 加冕（右下 SE）

The tide has pulled back: **wet stone steps rise toward the top of the panel**,
each tread ringed with a drying tide line, puddles left in the low corners.
Above them a broad platform of pale stone with an **empty stone seat at its
centre**, flanked by two low blocks (crown and seal), and the whale statues
again — but here they are **lower, half-submerged in the platform's edge**, all
bowing toward the seat. Warm light now: the cold silver of the previous panels
gives way to pale gold, with the strongest highlights on the seat and the wet
steps. Palette: wet stone #B9C4C6, pale gold #E8C87A, warm light #FFF3D6, silver
#C9CED8 (in the outermost corners only, to keep the transition from C).

## NEGATIVE（逐字保留）

Chinese or Latin text, lettering, watermark, logo, UI, frame, vignette,
photorealistic, glossy 3D, neon, high saturation, character, creature, person
