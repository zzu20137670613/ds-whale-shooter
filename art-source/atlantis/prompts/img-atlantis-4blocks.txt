> **"退潮刚露出来"的半淹状态**：水线、湿痕、露出半截的门都在画里，潮位留给我做。

## STYLE BIBLE（从 dsh6X6 三张提出来，逐字保留）

Palette — 深蓝绿 `#0B2E3A`、近黑礁 `#0A1519`、冷灰石 `#7E8C93`、青蓝高光
`#6FD3E8`、暗金 `#8A6B33`、水面反光 `#BFE9F0`、湿石 `#3E6B6E`。
整体低明度、冷色压全场，**靠一线冷青高光提亮**；暖色只有暗金，且只出现在王座/冠/印上。

Motif vocabulary — **鲸形石像**（俯视＝细长纺锤形石脊 + 尾鳍分叉，全部朝王座方向）、
铁链与辔链（俯视＝一串小亮点连成线，末端垂进水里）、石阶与台基（顶面平、边缘一圈湿痕）、
苔藓与珊瑚（低饱和的青灰团块，压在石缝里）、气泡与浮尘（细小白点，稀疏）、
裂纹穹顶（俯视＝不规则裂口，光从裂口以**落在地上的楔形光斑**出现）。

材质 — 石面湿、有均匀柔光反射（不是镜面高光）；水是**一层薄水**，下面的石板透出来、
被蓝色压一层；黑礁哑光、几乎没有反射。

## CAMERA（逐字保留）

True orthographic **plan view**, straight down. No horizon, no water surface seen
edge-on, no visible walls in elevation, no vanishing point. Water reads as **a
flat sheet with what lies beneath faintly visible**, never as a vertical wall of
water. A light shaft from a ceiling crack reads as **a hard wedge of light lying
on the floor**, never as a beam standing up. Steps read as **treads from above**.
Everything that stands (statues, throne, pillars) is drawn as its **top surface
plus a short shadow**, no side faces.

## EDGES（逐字保留）

Each image is one panel of a larger map. **The listed gradient edges must carry a
progressive colour transition over their outer 140 px**, ramping toward the
neighbour panel's dominant colour (given per panel), so the two panels meet in the
same colour family; the stitch pass lays a frosted-glass band over the seam, so the
ramp must be smooth, not banded.

The listed **hard edges are the map's outer boundary** and must terminate
naturally and readably — a wall of black reef, a stone parapet, open water falling
away — **not** fade to fog, and **not** be graded.

Keep the outer 140 px of every edge free of unique landmarks and readable detail.

## A · 海门 + 折断沉船（左下 SW）

Gradient: **top edge** ramps toward `#0A1822` (B is the darkest panel); **right
edge** ramps toward `#3A5A62` (D warms toward gold). Hard edges: left and bottom
terminate as a **black reef shelf** with open water beyond.

Content — drowned outskirts: fine pale seabed sand in soft ripples, drifting silt,
broken coral and rubble. Upper left, a **broken shipwreck** lies half-sunk: ribbed
hull timbers, a snapped mast, tattered sail fabric laid flat by water. To the
right the first **stone gate architecture**: a massive square doorway of pale cyan
stone, **half-open**, one door slab pushed inward at an angle, the gap reading as
pure deep blue `#10222E`. Barnacle crust and a drying tide line ring the gate's
base. Six or seven small bubbles drift near the sand.

## B · 深海之忆（左上 NW）

Gradient: **bottom edge** ramps toward `#16262E` (A's silt); **right edge** ramps
toward `#8FA3AC` (C's cold silver). Hard edges: top and left terminate against a
**wall of black reef**, no gradient.

Content — darkest panel. Seabed continues, now under a **thin sheet of still
water**, so stone slabs and rubble show through it, blue-shifted. At the centre a
**second stone door stands half-open**, and from its gap a **single narrow blade of
cold blue light** lies across the floor — the brightest thing here and the only
strong light. On the water surface, faint **crossed-out name marks** float as pale
scratches in loose rows like a drowned list — abstract glyph strokes, **no legible
characters**. Two broken sail-ribs and a coil of rotted rope lie near the door.

## C · 摄政之谏（右上 NE）

Gradient: **left edge** ramps toward `#2A424C` (B's deep water); **bottom edge**
ramps toward `#B9AD86` (D's pale gold light). Hard edges: top and right terminate
as a **stone parapet with black reef beyond**.

Content — a drowned hall, cold and still. Above, a **crack in the dome** — in plan
view an irregular jagged opening — lays a **wedge of pale sky light** across the
floor at an angle. The floor is **thin water like a mirror**: pillars and the
throne are reflected in it, soft and dim, with fine ripple rings where something
touched it. At the centre an **empty throne embedded in black reef**: pale stone
seat sunk in dark branching coral, with one line of dark-gold inlay on the back
seen from above. Around it, **whale-shaped stone statues** stand in a ring — long
tapered slabs with flukes, mossy, **all oriented toward the throne**, each casting
a short shadow inward. Three rings of abstract carved glyph marks mark three
question points on the floor (no real characters).

## D · 加冕（右下 SE）

Gradient: **left edge** ramps toward `#35505A` (A's cold water); **top edge** ramps
toward `#6E7F86` (C's wet stone). Hard edges: right and bottom terminate as a
**stone parapet over open water**.

Content — the tide has pulled back: **wet stone steps rise toward the top of the
panel**, each tread ringed with a drying tide line, shallow puddles left in the low
corners holding a little sky. Above them a broad platform of pale stone with an
**empty stone seat at its centre**, flanked by two low blocks (crown and seal),
each with the same dark-gold inlay line. The whale statues appear again, now
**lower, half-submerged at the platform edge**, all facing the seat. Warm light
takes over: cold silver gives way to pale gold on the wet steps and the seat, the
strongest highlights in the whole map.
