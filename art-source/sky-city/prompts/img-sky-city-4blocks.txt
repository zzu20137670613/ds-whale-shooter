# S2 天空之城 · 四块底图提示词（v2：加风格圣经 + 渐变边）

> 流程（`art-experiment/MULTIBLOCK-MAP-PIPELINE.md`）：
> 我写提示词 → 你出一张 2752×1536 → 我切 4 张 1376×768 → 你高清 2× →
> 我拼成 5504×3072（一块）→ 四块合成 4×4 屏（11008×6144）→ 接缝盖毛玻璃。
>
> **渐变必须出图时就做**（拼接时只盖毛玻璃）。毛玻璃参数定案：模糊半径 16 px、
> 带宽 ±48 px（= 3 × 半径）。所以下面每块都写清了"哪两条边渐变"。
>
> **风格圣经**取自 `art-experiment/gpt6X6/` 三张 galgame 背景（S2-1/2/3）。
> 那三张是**立绘式仰视**，不能当底图用，但配色/材质/纹样照它抄——**视角一律换成正俯视**。

## STYLE BIBLE（从 gpt6X6 三张提出来，逐字保留）

Palette — 云白 `#F4F8FC`、月白 `#E4EEF7`、淡金 `#E8D3A0`、天青 `#9EC6E8`、
云影冷灰 `#C3D2E2`、深蓝缝 `#2E4C6B`。整体高明度、低饱和，**上半部过曝到近白**，
只靠冷灰云影和金色的暖点撑层次。

Motif vocabulary — 白石栏杆与望柱（顶面有浅浮雕云纹）、斗拱牌楼（只画**顶面**
的层叠方木与瓦脊，绝不画侧面）、月牙纹与环形云纹（刻在石面上，线宽 1–2 px）、
垂下来的银链（俯视＝一串细小亮点连成线）、半透明的白纱/云带（叠在石面上，
边缘柔化）、月亮的位置用**光的圆形渐变**表示（不是画一个月亮在天上）。

材质 — 石面哑光、有细密凿痕；金件只在边缘反光；云是**地面雾**，有体积厚度、
能遮住下面的东西。

## CAMERA（逐字保留，沙滩 v1 就是漏了这段）

True orthographic **plan view**, straight down. No horizon, no sky band, no
vanishing point, no visible sides of anything standing above the ground plane.
Stairs read as **treads from above** (parallel lit edges with shadow gaps), never
as a staircase in elevation. Cloud reads as **ground fog from above** with the
ground substance showing through gaps. Verticality is expressed by the scene
rising toward the top of the frame, never by elevation. Pagodas and gates are
drawn as **roof planes seen from above** (ridge lines, tile courses, no walls).

## EDGES（逐字保留）

Each image is one panel of a larger map. **The listed gradient edges must carry a
progressive colour transition over their outer 140 px**, ramping toward the
neighbour panel's dominant colour (given per panel below), so the two panels meet
in the same colour family; the stitch pass will lay a frosted-glass band over the
seam, so the ramp must be smooth, not banded.

The listed **hard edges are the map's outer boundary** and must terminate
naturally and readably — a cliff of cloud, a stone balustrade, a drop into the
drowned sea — **not** fade to fog, and **not** be graded.

Keep the outer 140 px of every edge free of unique landmarks and readable detail.

## A · 月桥落点 + 云阶下段（左下 SW）

Gradient: **top edge** ramps toward `#F6F9FD` (B's blown-out white); **right edge**
ramps toward `#E7F1F9` (D's pale light). Hard edges: left and bottom terminate as
a **stone balustrade with a sheer drop into cloud**, and through the gaps far
below the sea shows as a thin deep-blue slit.

Content — a pale stone landing where a great arc of moonlit bridge comes down out
of the upper left, its far half swallowed by cloud; the bridge deck is drawn as
flat stone slabs with a carved lunar swirl. From the landing a **broad flight of
pale stone stairs climbs toward the top of the frame**, every tread lit on its
upper edge. Cloud banks drift across the stairs and part in lanes. Three small
broken bridge fragments rest near the stair edges (collectables). Palette stays
inside the style bible; the only cold note is the sea slit `#2E4C6B`.

## B · 云阶上段 + 云门（左上 NW）

Gradient: **bottom edge** ramps toward `#F4F8FC` (A's cloud white); **right edge**
ramps toward `#F0F4F0` (C's paper-and-slate grey). Hard edges: top and left
terminate as a **low balustrade dissolving into blown-out white light**.

Content — the stair flight continues off the bottom edge and ends at a **cloud
gate**: a squared gate with a wide tiled hip roof seen from above (ridge lines,
tile courses, dougong brackets only as a layered band under the eaves), its
pillars half-dissolved into cloud. Beyond it a flat stone terrace. Wind is
strongest here: long horizontal cloud streaks, loose debris pinned against the
pillars. Three circular **stepping stones** set into the terrace at irregular
spacing, each ringed with abstract carved glyph marks (no legible characters).
Brightest panel: highlights blown to near-white at the top.

## C · 城南老书阁（右上 NE）

Gradient: **left edge** ramps toward `#F0F4F0` (B's terrace); **bottom edge** ramps
toward `#EAF0F6` (D's pale light). Hard edges: top and right terminate as the
pavilion's **roof edge over open cloud** — the roof seen from above as a band of
tile courses along the top, then cloud, no walls.

Content — an old open-air book pavilion on slate paving: a square slate floor,
four corner pillar bases (round stone drums seen from above), the roof only along
the top edge. The wind stops here — **hundreds of loose pages hang suspended in
the air**, held flat and parallel to the ground, drifting in slow lanes, casting
soft grey shadows on the slate. At the centre an **enormous open book lies flat on
the floor**, two pages as flat cream fields with pale-gold edging, large enough to
stand on; the left page carries rows of abstract carved glyph marks (no real
characters). Three torn pages lie apart from the swarm.

## D · 王座之前（右下 SE）

Gradient: **left edge** ramps toward `#E7F1F9` (A's landing); **top edge** ramps
toward `#EAF0F6` (C's paving). Hard edges: right and bottom terminate into open
cloud — the platform simply ends.

Content — a **cloud-patterned empty throne** in a pool of light at the centre: a
blocky seat and back seen from above, surface carved with swirling cloud motifs
(flat paint, no relief modelled). From each armrest a **chain of silver links**
hangs and vanishes into the light (read from above as a fine line of bright
beads). Directly beneath the seat a **vertical shaft of light** breaks through the
cloud — in plan view a hard-edged bright disc with a soft halo, the brightest
thing in the map, with a ring of cloud torn open around it. Palette: near-white
`#FBFAF6`, pale gold `#F0D9A4`, silver `#C9CED8`; the outer corners may fall to a
deep prussian blue `#16324A` to set up S3.
