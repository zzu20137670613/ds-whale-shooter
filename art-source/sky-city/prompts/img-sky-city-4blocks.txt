## CAMERA（逐字保留）

True orthographic **plan view**, as if a drone pointed straight down at the
ground. No horizon, no sky band, no vanishing point, no visible sides of any
object taller than the ground plane. Stairs are read as **treads seen from
above** (parallel bands of lit edge + shadow gap), not as a staircase seen in
elevation. Clouds are **ground fog seen from above** — soft white cover that
hides whatever is under it, with the ground substance showing through gaps.
"Up" in the fiction is expressed by the scene rising toward the top of the
frame, never by a side elevation.

## STYLE（逐字保留）

Anime-inspired hand-painted 2D top-down game art, soft cel shading, clean
1–2 px line work on landmarks, matte texture (no glossy 3D render look), wide
flat areas of colour so characters and props remain readable on top. Palette
sampled from this block only, no foreign hues.

## SEAM（逐字保留）

This image is one of four panels of a larger map. Keep the **outer 120 px of
every edge free of unique landmarks, text, and hard shadow edges** — only
continuous ground texture, and let any structure that must continue (stairs,
floor tiles, cloud banks) run straight off the edge perpendicular to it. No
border, no frame, no vignette, no corner darkening.

---

## A · 月桥垂落点 + 云阶下段（左下）

Cloud city at its lowest: a pale stone landing where a great arch of moonlit
bridge comes down out of the upper left, its far half swallowed by cloud. From
the landing a **broad flight of pale stone stairs climbs toward the top of the
frame**, every tread lit along its upper edge and shadowed under its lip.
Cloud banks drift across the stairs and part in lanes. Through two gaps in the
cloud, far below, the sea is visible as a **thin deep-blue slit** — the only
cold colour in the panel. Scattered pale-gold cloud wisps and three small
broken pieces of the moon bridge rest on the steps (collectables, small, near
the stair edges). Palette: cloud white #F5F2EA, pale gold #E8C87A, stone
#DED6C6, shadow #8B8CA6, the sea slit #1B3A57 only.

## B · 云阶上段 + 云门（左上）

The top of the stair flight continues off the left edge and ends at a **cloud
gate**: two squared pillars of pale stone half-dissolved into cloud, a lintel
across them, and beyond the gate a flat terrace of stone. Here the wind is
strongest — cloud streams are long horizontal streaks, and small loose debris
sits pinned against the pillars. Three circular **stepping stones** are set
into the terrace at irregular spacing ("the three questions"), each with a
carved ring of unreadable glyph marks (abstract carved lines, **not legible
characters**). Brightest panel of the four: highlights blown out to near-white
at the top, ground still readable. Palette: white #F7F4EC dominating, pale gold
#EFD79B, stone #E3DCCE, cool shadow #9A9BB2.

## C · 城南老书阁（右上）

An old open-air book pavilion on a stone terrace, seen from above: a square
slate floor, four corner pillars, no roof (or a roof only over the far edge,
seen as flat tile). The wind stops here — **hundreds of loose pages hang
suspended in the air**, held flat and parallel to the ground, drifting in slow
lanes, casting soft grey shadows on the slate. At the centre an **enormous open
book lies flat on the floor**, its two pages readable as flat coloured fields
(cream paper, faint pale-gold edging), big enough to stand on; the left page
carries rows of **carved unreadable glyph marks** (abstract, no real
characters). Three torn pages lie apart from the swarm (collectables). Palette:
slate #B9B6AE, paper cream #F2E9D2, page shadow #8E8A7E, pale gold #E0C98F.

## D · 王座之前（右下）

No walls, no floor edge — the ground simply ends into cloud on three sides. A
**cloud-patterned empty throne** floats in a pool of light at the centre: a
blocky seat and back seen from above, its surface carved with swirling cloud
motifs (flat, painted, no relief). From each armrest a **chain of silver links
hangs down** and disappears into the light. Directly beneath the seat a
**vertical shaft of light** breaks through the cloud — in plan view this reads
as a hard-edged bright disc with a soft halo, the brightest thing in the whole
map, with a ring of cloud torn open around it. Palette: near-white #FBFAF6,
pale gold #F0D9A4, silver #C9CED8, shaft halo #FFF6DE; the outer corners may
fall to a deep prussian blue #16324A (this panel turns dark at its south edge,
setting up S3).

## NEGATIVE（逐字保留）

sky gradient band, visible walls in elevation, staircase seen from the side,
readable Chinese or Latin text, lettering, watermark, logo, UI, frame border,
vignette, photorealistic, glossy 3D render, neon, high saturation, character,
creature, person, hands
