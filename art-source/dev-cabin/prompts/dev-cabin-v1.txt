# 隐藏关 3 · 开发者小屋（单图 2752x1536）· v1

> 规格抄的是**酒馆那张**（`public/assets/image/scenes/tavern.png`）——这边"单图小屋"的标准：
> **严格正俯视、地板铺满整张画、家具一律贴边、没有立起来的墙**。
>
> 三个设计要点：
>
> 1. **笔记本是开着的、屏幕朝镜头倾斜**。从正上方看显示器本来只能看到一块板，
>    但开着的笔记本屏幕天然朝上倾斜 —— 这样"屏幕亮着、能点"在物理上成立，
>    玩家也一眼知道那是可交互的东西。
> 2. **那个人只给背影**：小、弓在桌前、脸被头发和椅背挡住。
>    正面画脸必崩，而且这屋子的重点是"她在写代码"，不是她长什么样。
> 3. **不往画面里塞字**。破第四面墙靠互动不靠画面——模型画文字一律出乱码。

```
2D top-down game map asset seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE (90 degrees, plan view), one cozy cabin room. The worn plank floor fills the whole frame and runs past ALL FOUR edges: no rim, no border, no vignette. Flat cel shading, thin ink outlines, large smooth fills - NOT painterly, NOT photoreal.

NO standing walls, no ceiling, no roof beams, no window in elevation: the room's edges are flat wooden bands and rails only.

FURNITURE, all touching the edges, never the middle: along the TOP EDGE a long worktable seen from above; LEFT EDGE a low shelf with books and a whiteboard; RIGHT EDGE the corner of a bunk bed and a beanbag.

ON THE TABLE, the centrepiece: an OPEN LAPTOP seen from above with its lid tilted toward the camera, so the glowing screen is clearly visible - a bright cyan-white panel with faint lines of code. Beside it a small flat monitor, a keyboard, a mouse, a mug, noodle cups, a rubber duck, sticky notes along the table edge, a tangle of cables.

ONE PERSON, small: seen from above and behind, hunched at the table on an office chair, back to the camera, one hand on the mouse. Face hidden by hair and the chair back. Dark hoodie, headphones. One readable silhouette, no detail.

FLAT PROPS on the planks: printed pages, a whale plush on the shelf, an energy drink, a plush cat asleep on the beanbag, a whiteboard with a rough diagram, a potted plant.

LIGHT: 3am. A warm desk lamp pool over the table, cold screen glow spilling onto the planks, the rest of the room in deep blue shadow, rain at the very edge. Long soft shadows.

Palette: warm oak #8A5A33, plank shadow #4A2E1C, screen cyan #7FE6F0, lamp gold #F0B45C, deep night blue #1A2233, dust grey #9A9186.

Size: 16:9, full bleed, composed for a 2752x1536 canvas (readable inside the central 90%).

Negative: isometric, three-quarter, perspective, vanishing point, walls standing, ceiling, roof, window frame in elevation, horizon, crowd, text, letters, watermark, logo, clutter in the centre, daylight, painterly.
```
