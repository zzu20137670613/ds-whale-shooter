CAMERA — this is a game level background seen from directly above.
- Camera high overhead, almost straight down: a top-down game-map view, like a floor plan.
- ORTHOGRAPHIC: no perspective at all. Parallel lines stay parallel; the walkway bands stay the
  same width from one edge of the image to the other; no vanishing point, no converging lines, no
  foreshortening.
- EVERY VISIBLE SURFACE IS HORIZONTAL. The water surface, the walkways and the paper are all
  seen as flat tops. No side of a walkway, no visible thickness of the paper. If you can see the
  height of any object, the view is wrong.
- NO ARCHITECTURE WITH HEIGHT ANYWHERE: no arches, no columns, no doorways, no steps, no raised
  walls, no bookshelves, no balustrades, no platforms. This room is a flat floor with flat
  patterns inlaid in it — nothing to climb.
- No horizon line anywhere.

SPACE BUDGET — THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT REQUIREMENT. An earlier attempt failed because the
furniture ate the playable area.
- THE WATER IS WALKABLE FLOOR. This is the key point: the mirror water is only ankle-deep over a
  stone floor — the player walks straight across it. It is a reflective FLOOR, not a hazard, not
  a pit, not a wall. It must be open and continuous across at least 80% of the image.
- The stone walkways are FLUSH with the water — barely raised kerbs a few centimetres high, so
  they are walkable decoration, never obstacles you have to climb.
- Everything that blocks movement (the great scroll, the void rim) together: at most 20%.
- The great scroll: the one large obstacle, a long straight VERTICAL wall, about 10% of the image,
  placed toward one side. It must NOT cut the image into two disconnected halves — leave wide
  walkable lanes of open water on BOTH sides of it — and it must never span the image
  horizontally or diagonally. Everything else stays open.
- A player must be able to walk between any two objects: leave wide open lanes everywhere.
- No large dark masses anywhere.

EXPOSURE — this background is played on, so it must be clearly readable. Hard requirements:
- This is the darkest area of the level, but it must still be CLEARLY READABLE — deep blue, never
  murky, muddy or dim. Overall brightness around 75-95/255.
- At least half of the image sits in the mid-tone range (roughly #4a5a7c to #c8d0e0).
- No more than about 20% of the image may be darker than #2a3a58, and none of it may be pure
  black. Near-black only in small contact shadows.
- Every edge — where water meets stone, where a walkway ends, where the paper lies on the water —
  must be crisp and easy to read. No soft murky falloff.
- NO CAST SHADOWS: do not paint any directional shadow across the water from the scroll, the
  walkways or the kerbs — no long dark bands at all.
- EVEN LIGHTING — a hard requirement: the water and the walkways are lit uniformly across the
  entire image, with no bright pool and no dark corner. Any two patches of open water anywhere
  should read within about 2x of each other, and the image's MEDIAN brightness should land close
  to its average (a median far below the mean means one spotlight in a dark room, which is wrong
  here).
- The water must stay readable everywhere: never below about #1e2e4d, with clearly visible star
  reflections; the stone walkways never below #9e9fb8.

THE WATER — the subject of this image: ankle-deep, mirror-calm water lying over a pale stone

THE WALKWAYS: pale stone walkways (#b9c2d6) laid out in a loose grid as flat MOSAIC PATTERNS
INLAID in the floor at exactly the same level as the water — water and stone are two flat

LANDMARK (well inside the image, never touching an edge): one colossal unfurled scroll of bright

NOT run diagonally. Both ends are rolled: seen from directly above they are flat spirals, not
cylinders. The paper carries blank, illegible rows of marks — no readable letters — and a single

A thin strip of deep blue starfield with clearly visible stars runs along the LOWER border and the
LEFT border only, where the vault edge falls away into the void — those two sides are the outer

walkways: a neighbouring image continues the same floor there.

LIGHTING: everything is blue and cool, but never dark or muddy: the water, kerbs, walkways and
the paper are all clearly readable, lit evenly across the whole image. A faint warm tint on the
empty signature line is the one warm accent — a colour change on the paper, not a spotlight on
the floor.

RULES
- Top-down only. No horizon line, no perspective, no side view, no ceiling.
- Keep the water open and shallow: at least four fifths of the image must be walkable water or
  walkway, not obstacle.
- Every material, wall, floor and light source must run straight off the image edge. Nothing
  may stop at the edge, fade out at the edge, or be centred on the edge.
- No characters, no people, no creatures, no text, no letters, no numbers, no legible writing,
  no UI, no watermark, no frame, no border, no vignette, no signature.
- Keep the composition generic and continuous; the one landmark above is the only focal point.
- On the two sides NOT marked as the map rim, a neighbouring image must be able to continue the
  same water, walkways and kerbs seamlessly.
