CAMERA — this is a game level background seen from directly above.
- Camera high overhead, almost straight down: a top-down game-map view, like a floor plan.
- ORTHOGRAPHIC: no perspective at all. Parallel lines stay parallel; the shelf runs and the plank
  lines stay straight and evenly spaced from one edge of the image to the other; no vanishing
  point, no converging lines, no foreshortening.
- EVERY VISIBLE SURFACE IS HORIZONTAL. No vertical surface may be visible anywhere: a bookshelf
  is only its flat TOP, never the row of book spines on a vertical face; no side of a bench, no
  visible thickness of a book stack. If you can see the height of anything, the view is wrong.
- NO ARCHITECTURE WITH HEIGHT: no steps, no standing ladders, no railings, no arches, no columns,
  no balustrades, no raised platforms. Nothing to climb, nothing to look up at.
- NOTHING IS DIAGONAL: every shelf run, every plank line and the room boundary run exactly
  parallel to the image edges and meet at clean right angles. This is a floor plan.
- No horizon line, no ceiling, no cast shadows anywhere.

SPACE BUDGET — the most important requirement. An earlier attempt failed because the furniture ate
the playable area.
- OPEN WALKABLE FLOOR: at least 75% of the image — one wide continuous floor the player can run
  across in any direction.
- Bookshelves: LOW flat shelves seen from above as thin pale rectangles, in a LOOSE grid with wide
  aisles. All shelves together: at most 20% of the image. Never a solid wall of shelves, never a
  cluster, never one shelf longer than about a sixth of the image.
- Leave wide open lanes everywhere: the player must be able to run between any two shelves.
- No large dark masses anywhere.

EXPOSURE — this background is played on, so it must be clearly readable.
- Mid-tones dominate. Overall brightness around 100 to 130 of 255: a cool night scene that is
  still CLEARLY LIT, never a dark painting.
- At least half of the image sits in the mid-tone range.
- No more than about 15% of the image may be darker than #3a4258, and none of it may be pure
  black.
- EVEN LIGHTING, a hard requirement: the floor is lit uniformly across the whole image, with no
  bright pool and no dark corner. Any two patches of open floor should read within about 2x of
  each other, and the image's MEDIAN brightness should land close to its average (a median far
  below the mean means one spotlight in a dark room, which is wrong here).
- NO CAST SHADOWS: no directional shadow on the floor from any shelf, bench or book stack.

PALETTE, measured from the reference sheets:
- LEFT half, the warm room: polished dark wood floor, #865340 mid to #a8805f highlight, long
  straight planks; brass and warm amber accents; a few pale paper-white #f2eee9 details.
- RIGHT half, the cool maze: the same plank floor in cool blue-grey, #4a5a78 mid to #6b7c9c
  highlight; pale beige shelf tops #c8b48f with thin brass edging.
- Both halves share the same cool moonlight fill so the two palettes meet softly instead of
  clashing.

STYLE: soft semi-realistic anime background painting, painterly, clean readable shapes.

One image, two rooms, one soft boundary, seen from directly above.

THE LEFT HALF (a warm library room): open polished wood plank floor in warm brown, with one or two
low flat benches and two or three flat stacks of books. This is the right-hand part of a warm
lending-counter room; keep it mostly empty floor.

THE RIGHT HALF (a cool shelf maze): the same plank floor in cool blue-grey, crossed by LOW flat
bookshelves in a loose grid with wide aisles, plus a few flat stacks of books. This is the
left-hand part of a vast shelf maze.

THE BOUNDARY: the two rooms meet along a SOFT VERTICAL BAND running straight down the MIDDLE of
the image, from the top edge to the bottom edge, parallel to the left and right edges. It is a
gentle transition roughly a quarter of the image wide — warm floor fading into cool floor. Not a
hard line, not a wall, not a doorway, not an arch. Nothing is built on the boundary.

THE TOP EDGE, the only outer edge: along the top border only, a THIN flat band about 6% of the
image of LOW bookshelf tops runs straight and parallel to the top border — warm wood and brass on
the left half, pale beige shelves on the right half — seen from directly above as flat rectangles.
Beyond that band the floor simply continues to the top edge. Do NOT paint any starfield, void,
railing or sky at the top. The other three borders, left, right and bottom, are plain continuing
floor with nothing on them: neighbouring images continue the same two rooms there.

LANDMARK: none. This is a passage image, so keep it open. Two or three flat stacks of books and
one or two low benches only, each a couple of percent of the image at most.

RULES
- Top-down only. No horizon line, no perspective, no side view, no ceiling, no walls with height.
- Check that at least three quarters of the image is unobstructed walkable floor before finishing.
- Every material and every line runs straight off the image edge; nothing may stop at the edge,
  fade out at the edge, or be centred on the edge.
- No characters, no people, no creatures, no text, no letters, no numbers, no legible writing, no
  UI, no watermark, no frame, no border, no vignette, no signature.
- The composition stays generic and continuous: no focal point, no hero object.
