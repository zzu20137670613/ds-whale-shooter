CAMERA — this is a game level background seen from directly above.
- Camera high overhead, almost straight down: a top-down game-map view, like a floor plan.
- ORTHOGRAPHIC: no perspective at all. Parallel lines stay parallel; the plank lines, the walkway
  bands and the water's shoreline stay straight and evenly spaced from one edge of the image to
  the other; no vanishing point, no converging lines, no foreshortening.
- EVERY VISIBLE SURFACE IS HORIZONTAL. No vertical surface may be visible anywhere: no side of a
  walkway, no edge with height, no thickness of a book stack, no elevation of the stone kerb. If
  you can see the height of anything, the view is wrong.
- NO ARCHITECTURE WITH HEIGHT: no railings, no vertical posts, no balusters, no steps, no arches,
  no columns, no pedestals, no raised platforms. Nothing to climb, nothing to look up at.
- NOTHING IS DIAGONAL: every plank line, every walkway band, the water boundary and the stone kerb
  run exactly parallel to the image edges and meet at clean right angles. This is a floor plan.
- No horizon line, no ceiling, no cast shadows anywhere.

SPACE BUDGET — the most important requirement. An earlier attempt failed because the furniture ate
the playable area.
- OPEN AREA: at least 75% of the image is open ground the player can cross in any direction. The
  water in the lower half is WALKABLE FLOOR, not a hazard: it is ankle-deep mirror-calm water over
  the same stone floor, and the player runs across it.
- Everything that blocks movement (stone kerb band, benches, walkway edging, slabs, book stacks)
  together: at most 25% of the image.
- The stone kerb on the left is a THIN narrow band, about 6% of the image, and nothing else stands
  on the left border.
- Leave wide open lanes everywhere: the player must be able to cross the whole image without
  squeezing between two objects.
- No large dark masses anywhere.

EXPOSURE — this background is played on, so it must be clearly readable.
- Mid-tones dominate. Overall image brightness around 90 to 125 of 255: a cool night scene that is
  still CLEARLY LIT, never a dark painting.
- The two halves have DIFFERENT natural brightness and that is intended: the warm wood half is the
  brighter one, the water half is cooler and darker. Both must be evenly lit WITHIN themselves —
  no bright pool and no dark corner inside either half. Do not brighten the water until it turns
  grey: it stays deep blue, and it stays readable.
- No more than about 15% of the image may be darker than #3a4258, and none of it may be pure
  black. The water never falls below about #1e2e4d anywhere.
- At least half of the image sits in the mid-tone range.
- NO CAST SHADOWS: no directional shadow on the floor or on the water from any bench, slab, stack
  or kerb.

PALETTE, measured from the reference sheets:
- Upper half, the warm room: polished dark wood floor, #865340 mid to #a8805f highlight, long
  straight planks; brass and warm amber accents; paper white #f2eee9 details.
- Lower half, the flooded room: mirror-calm deep blue water #1e2e4d to #2b3f66 with clearly
  visible reflected stars; pale stone walkways #b9c2d6; wet pale slabs; paper white details.
- Left border: pale stone kerb #c7c1cf, then starfield #273559 to #3b4a74 with clearly visible
  stars, then a flat dark sea surface far away.
- Both halves share the same cool moonlight fill so the warm and the cool meet softly.

STYLE: soft semi-realistic anime background painting, painterly, clean readable shapes.

One image, two rooms, one soft boundary, seen from directly above.

THE UPPER HALF (a warm library room): open polished wood plank floor in warm brown, with one or two
low flat benches and two or three flat stacks of books. This is the lower part of a warm
lending-counter room; keep it mostly empty floor.

THE LOWER HALF (a flooded mirror room): mirror-calm shallow water over the same stone floor,
reflecting stars, crossed by FLAT pale stone walkways INLAID at exactly the same level as the
water — water and stone are two flat surfaces side by side, like tiles in one floor. No steps, no
raised edges, no kerbs with height, no platforms, nothing to climb. A few flat wet slabs and two
or three flat stacks of books. Mostly open water.

THE BOUNDARY: the two rooms meet along a SOFT HORIZONTAL BAND running straight across the MIDDLE of
the image, from the left edge to the right edge, parallel to the top and bottom edges. It is a
gentle transition roughly a quarter of the image tall — warm planks fading into water. Not a hard
line, not a wall, not a step, not a doorway, not an arch. Nothing is built on the boundary.

THE LEFT EDGE, the only outer edge: running straight down the left border, parallel to it, a THIN
flat band about 6% of the image of pale stone kerb, seen from directly above as a flat strip with a
line of small FLAT carved squares — NEVER a railing, NEVER a row of vertical posts or balusters,
nothing whose height you could see. Beyond it a strip of deep blue starfield (#273559 to #3b4a74)
with clearly visible stars, and far away a flat dark sea surface, both seen from directly above with
no horizon line. This band runs the full height of the image, through both halves. The TOP border,
the RIGHT border and the BOTTOM border carry NOTHING at all: plain continuing floor and plain
continuing water to the very edge, because neighbouring images continue both rooms there.

LANDMARK: none. This is a passage image, so keep it open. Two or three flat stacks of books, one or
two low benches and a few flat wet slabs only, each a couple of percent of the image at most.

RULES
- Top-down only. No horizon line, no perspective, no side view, no ceiling, no walls with height.
- Check that at least three quarters of the image is open crossable floor and water before
  finishing.
- Every material and every line runs straight off the image edge; nothing may stop at the edge,
  fade out at the edge, or be centred on the edge.
- No characters, no people, no creatures, no text, no letters, no numbers, no legible writing, no
  UI, no watermark, no frame, no border, no vignette, no signature.
- The composition stays generic and continuous: no focal point, no hero object.
