EXPOSURE — this background is played on, so it must be clearly readable. Hard requirements:
- Mid-tones dominate. Overall brightness around 100/255: a cool but CLEARLY LIT scene, not a dark
  scene, not a mood painting.
- At least half of the image sits in the mid-tone range (roughly #5a6a88 to #d8dce8).
- No more than about 15% of the image may be darker than #3a4258, and none of it may be pure
  black. Near-black only in small contact shadows.
- The floor must read as lit across the WHOLE image, not only in bright spots.
- NO CAST SHADOWS: do not paint any directional shadow on the marble from the balustrade, the
  pedestals or the benches — no long dark diagonal bands at all. Short soft contact shadows
  directly under an object are the only shadows allowed.
- EVEN LIGHTING — a hard requirement: the marble is lit uniformly across the entire image, with
  no bright pool and no dark corner. Any two patches of open floor anywhere should read within
  about 2x of each other, and the image's MEDIAN brightness should land close to its average
  (a median far below the mean means one spotlight in a dark room, which is wrong here).
- The marble must stay bright everywhere: never below about #9e9fb8 anywhere in the image,
  including the far corners. Only gentle falloff.

RULES
