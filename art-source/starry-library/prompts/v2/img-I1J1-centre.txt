CAMERA — this is a game level background seen from directly above.
- Camera high overhead, almost straight down: a top-down game-map view, like a floor plan.
- ORTHOGRAPHIC: no perspective at all. Parallel lines stay parallel; the plank lines, the shelf
  runs, the marble joint lines, the walkway bands and the water's edge stay straight and evenly
  spaced from one edge of the image to the other; no vanishing point, no converging lines.
- EVERY VISIBLE SURFACE IS HORIZONTAL. No vertical surface may be visible anywhere: a bookshelf is
  only its flat TOP, never the row of book spines on a vertical face; no side of a bench or a
  table, no visible thickness of a slab. If you can see the height of anything, the view is wrong.
- NO ARCHITECTURE WITH HEIGHT: no railings, no vertical posts, no balusters, no steps, no arches,
  no columns, no pedestals, no raised platforms. Nothing to climb, nothing to look up at.
- NOTHING IS DIAGONAL: every line in this image runs exactly parallel to the top, bottom, left and
  right edges and meets at clean right angles. The two room boundaries in particular are perfectly
  straight and perfectly vertical and horizontal. This is a floor plan, not a perspective drawing.
- No horizon line, no ceiling, no cast shadows anywhere. Render at high resolution: crisp plank
  grain, thin inlaid lines, clean carved patterns, sharp fine detail.

SPACE BUDGET — the most important requirement. An earlier attempt failed because the furniture ate
the playable area.
- OPEN AREA: at least 75% of the image is open ground the player can cross in any direction. The
  water in the lower-left quarter is WALKABLE FLOOR, not a hazard: it is ankle-deep mirror-calm
  water over stone, and the player runs across it.
- Bookshelves exist ONLY in the upper-right quarter: LOW flat shelves seen from above as thin pale
  rectangles, in a loose grid with wide aisles. All shelves together: at most 15% of the image.
  Never a solid wall of shelves, never a cluster.
- Everything else is small and sparse: two low flat benches in the upper-left quarter, one small
  round table in the lower-right quarter, two flat slabs, two or three flat stacks of books.
- Nothing may sit on or near a room boundary, and nothing may sit at the centre of the image.
- Leave wide open lanes everywhere: the player must be able to cross the whole image without
  squeezing between two objects.
- No large dark masses anywhere.

EXPOSURE — this background is played on, so it must be clearly readable.
- Mid-tones dominate. Overall image brightness around 110 to 145 of 255: a cool night scene that is
  still CLEARLY LIT, never a dark painting.
- The four quarters have DIFFERENT natural brightness and that is intended: the marble quarter is
  the brightest, the wood quarter is warm and mid, the shelf quarter is cooler and a little darker,
  the water quarter is the darkest. All four must be evenly lit WITHIN themselves — no bright pool
  and no dark corner inside any quarter. Do not flatten them to one brightness; the contrast
  between the quarters is what makes this image read.
- No more than about 15% of the image may be darker than #3a4258, and none of it may be pure
  black. The water never falls below about #1e2e4d anywhere.
- At least half of the image sits in the mid-tone range.
- NO CAST SHADOWS: no directional shadow on any floor or on the water from any shelf, bench, table,
  slab or stack.

STYLE: soft semi-realistic anime background painting, painterly, clean readable shapes.

One image, four rooms meeting at one point, seen from directly above.

THE UPPER-LEFT QUARTER (a warm library room): open polished wood plank floor in warm brown, with
two low flat benches and two flat stacks of books. Mostly empty floor.

THE UPPER-RIGHT QUARTER (a cool shelf maze): the same plank floor in cool blue-grey, crossed by LOW
flat bookshelves in a loose grid with wide aisles, plus one flat stack of books.

THE LOWER-RIGHT QUARTER (an open marble terrace): wide bright unobstructed marble floor with faint
inlaid star-map circles and long straight etched lines, one small round table seen from directly
above as a flat disc, and two flat slabs.

THE LOWER-LEFT QUARTER (a flooded mirror room): mirror-calm shallow water over stone, reflecting
stars, crossed by FLAT pale stone walkways INLAID at exactly the same level as the water — water
and stone are two flat surfaces side by side, like tiles in one floor. No steps, no raised edges,
nothing to climb.

THE TWO BOUNDARIES: the four quarters meet along TWO SOFT BANDS that cross at the EXACT CENTRE of
the image — one perfectly VERTICAL band running from the top edge to the bottom edge down the middle
of the image, and one perfectly HORIZONTAL band running from the left edge to the right edge across
the middle. Each band is a gentle transition roughly a quarter of the image wide, where one material
fades into the next. They must cross at the exact centre of the image, and they must be straight and
parallel to the image edges. Not hard black lines, not walls, not steps, not doorways, not arches.
Nothing is built on either boundary, and nothing sits at the crossing point.

THE FOUR EDGES: every border of this image is an INNER edge of the map, so all four borders carry
NOTHING at all — no kerb, no stone band, no railing, no starfield, no void, no sky, no sea. Each
border is plain continuing material, split at its midpoint: the top border is warm wood on its left
half and cool plank floor on its right half; the bottom border is mirror water on its left half and
plain marble on its right half; the left border is warm wood on its upper half and mirror water on
its lower half; the right border is cool plank floor on its upper half and plain marble on its lower
half. Neighbouring images continue all four of these rooms.

LANDMARK: none. This is the crossing point of the level, so keep it open and even. Two low benches,
one small flat round table, two flat slabs and three flat stacks of books only, each a couple of
percent of the image at most, and none of them anywhere near a boundary or the centre.

RULES
- Top-down only. No horizon line, no perspective, no side view, no ceiling, no walls with height.
- The two boundaries must cross at the exact centre of the image: check this before finishing.
- Check that at least three quarters of the image is open crossable floor and water before
  finishing.
- Every material and every line runs straight off the image edge; nothing may stop at the edge,
  fade out at the edge, or be centred on the edge.
- No characters, no people, no creatures, no text, no letters, no numbers, no legible writing, no
  UI, no watermark, no frame, no border, no vignette, no signature.
- The composition stays generic and continuous: no focal point, no hero object.
