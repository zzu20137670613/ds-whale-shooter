CAMERA — this is a game level background seen from directly above.
- Camera high overhead, almost straight down: a top-down game-map view, like a floor plan.
- ORTHOGRAPHIC: no perspective at all. Parallel lines stay parallel; the plank and marble joint
  lines, the walkway bands, the water's edge and the stone kerb stay straight and evenly spaced
  from one edge of the image to the other; no vanishing point, no converging lines.
- EVERY VISIBLE SURFACE IS HORIZONTAL. No vertical surface may be visible anywhere: no side of a
  walkway, no edge with height, no thickness of a table or a slab, no elevation of the stone kerb.
  If you can see the height of anything, the view is wrong.
- NO ARCHITECTURE WITH HEIGHT: no railings, no vertical posts, no balusters, no steps, no arches,
  no columns, no pedestals, no raised platforms. Nothing to climb, nothing to look up at.
- NOTHING IS DIAGONAL: every joint line, every walkway band, the water's edge and the stone kerb
  run exactly parallel to the image edges and meet at clean right angles. This is a floor plan.
- No horizon line, no ceiling, no cast shadows anywhere. Render at high resolution: crisp thin
  inlaid lines, clean carved patterns, sharp fine detail.

SPACE BUDGET — the most important requirement. An earlier attempt failed because the furniture ate
the playable area.
- OPEN AREA: at least 75% of the image is open ground the player can cross in any direction. The
  water in the left half is WALKABLE FLOOR, not a hazard: it is ankle-deep mirror-calm water over
  the same stone floor, and the player runs across it.
- The marble in the right half is furniture: keep it almost entirely bare — at most one small table
  seen from above as a flat disc, and two flat slabs.
- Everything that blocks movement (stone kerb band, table, slabs, walkway edging, book stacks)
  together: at most 25% of the image.
- The stone kerb on the bottom is a THIN narrow band, about 6% of the image, and nothing else
  stands on the bottom border.
- Leave wide open lanes everywhere: the player must be able to cross the whole image without
  squeezing between two objects.
- No large dark masses anywhere.

EXPOSURE — this background is played on, so it must be clearly readable.
- Mid-tones dominate. Overall image brightness around 110 to 145 of 255: a cool night scene that is
  still CLEARLY LIT, never a dark painting.
- The two halves have different natural brightness and that is intended: the marble half is the
  brighter one, the water half is cooler and darker. Both must be evenly lit WITHIN themselves — no
  bright pool and no dark corner inside either half. Do not brighten the water until it turns grey,
  and do not darken the marble to match it: the water stays deep blue and readable.
- No more than about 15% of the image may be darker than #3a4258, and none of it may be pure
  black. The water never falls below about #1e2e4d anywhere.
- At least half of the image sits in the mid-tone range.
- NO CAST SHADOWS: no directional shadow on the marble or on the water from any table, slab or
  kerb.

PALETTE, measured from the reference sheets:
- Left half, the flooded room: mirror-calm deep blue water #1e2e4d to #2b3f66 with clearly visible
  reflected stars; pale stone walkways #b9c2d6; wet pale slabs; paper white #f2eee9 details.
- Right half, the marble terrace: cool ivory marble #f0ece7 highlight, #c7c1cf mid, #9e9fb8 shadow,
  with faint inlaid star-map circles and long straight etched lines; pale blue moonlight fill
  #8fb0d8.
- Bottom border: pale stone kerb #c7c1cf, then starfield #273559 to #3b4a74 with clearly visible
  stars, then a flat dark sea surface far away.
- Both halves share the same cool moonlight fill so the two materials meet softly.

STYLE: soft semi-realistic anime background painting, painterly, clean readable shapes.

One image, two rooms, one soft boundary, seen from directly above.

THE LEFT HALF (the flooded mirror room): mirror-calm shallow water over stone, reflecting stars,
crossed by FLAT pale stone walkways INLAID at exactly the same level as the water — water and stone
are two flat surfaces side by side, like tiles in one floor. No steps, no raised edges, no kerbs
with height, no platforms, nothing to climb. A few flat wet slabs and two or three flat stacks of
books. Mostly open water. This is the lower part of the flooded room with the colossal scroll.

THE RIGHT HALF (the marble terrace): a wide bright unobstructed marble floor with faint inlaid
star-map circles and long straight etched lines, plus one small round table seen from directly
above as a flat disc and two flat slabs. This is the lower-left part of an open marble terrace.

THE BOUNDARY: the two rooms meet along a SOFT VERTICAL BAND running straight down the MIDDLE of the
image, from the top edge to the bottom edge, parallel to the left and right edges. It is a gentle
transition roughly a quarter of the image wide — mirror water fading into pale dry marble, like the
wet edge of a flood. Not a hard black line, not a wall, not a step, not a doorway, not an arch.
Nothing is built on the boundary.

THE BOTTOM EDGE, the only outer edge: running straight along the bottom border, parallel to it, a
THIN flat band about 6% of the image of pale stone kerb, seen from directly above as a flat strip
with a line of small FLAT carved squares — NEVER a railing, NEVER a row of vertical posts or
balusters, nothing whose height you could see. Beyond it a strip of deep blue starfield (#273559 to
#3b4a74) with clearly visible stars, and far away a flat dark sea surface, both seen from directly
above with no horizon line. This band runs the full width of the image, through both halves: beside
the water it reads as a low wet stone edge, beside the marble as a clean carved kerb. The TOP
border, the LEFT border and the RIGHT border carry NOTHING at all: plain continuing water on the
left and plain continuing marble on the right, to the very edge, because neighbouring images
continue both rooms there.

LANDMARK: none. This is a passage image, so keep it open. One small flat round table, two flat slabs
and two or three flat stacks of books only, each a couple of percent of the image at most.

RULES
- Top-down only. No horizon line, no perspective, no side view, no ceiling, no walls with height.
- Check that at least three quarters of the image is open crossable floor and water before
  finishing.
- Every material and every line runs straight off the image edge; nothing may stop at the edge,
  fade out at the edge, or be centred on the edge.
- No characters, no people, no creatures, no text, no letters, no numbers, no legible writing, no
  UI, no watermark, no frame, no border, no vignette, no signature.
- The composition stays generic and continuous: no focal point, no hero object.
