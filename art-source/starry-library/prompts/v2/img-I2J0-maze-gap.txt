EXPOSURE — this background is played on, so it must be clearly readable. Hard requirements:
- Mid-tones dominate. Overall brightness around 100/255: a dim but CLEARLY LIT scene, not a dark
  scene, not a mood painting.
- At least half of the image sits in the mid-tone range (roughly #5a6a88 to #d8dce8).
- No more than about 15% of the image may be darker than #3a4258, and none of it may be pure
  black. Near-black only in small contact shadows.
- The floor must read as lit across the WHOLE image, not only in bright spots. Cool blue ambient
  moonlight fills the space so that even the far corners stay visible.
- NO CAST SHADOWS: do not paint any directional shadow on the floor from the shelves, the ladders
  or the benches — no long dark diagonal bands at all. Short soft contact shadows directly under
  an object are the only shadows allowed.
- EVEN LIGHTING — a hard requirement: the floor is lit uniformly across the entire image, with
  no bright pool and no dark corner. Any two patches of open floor anywhere should read within
  about 2x of each other, and the image's MEDIAN brightness should land close to its average
  (a median far below the mean means one spotlight in a dark room, which is wrong here).
- The floor must never fall below about #4a5878 (luminance about 75/255) anywhere in the image,
  including the far corners. Only gentle falloff; cool moonlight fill keeps everything readable.
