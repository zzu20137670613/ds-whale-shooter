# Prop prompt — the iced juice glass (region-trigger reward)
#
# What it is for: the beach lounger easter egg. Stepping on the lounger pops a
# glass of iced juice out of the seat; it arcs onto the side table and waits
# there. Walking into it refills HP and SP.
#
# Where it goes when it arrives: public/assets/image/drops/iced-juice.png,
# wired by a new `public/data/drops/iced-juice.yaml` + one line in
# public/data/drops/index.yaml, and then `triggers[0].spawn.type` in
# public/data/levels/beach-holiday.yaml switches from `beach-drink` to
# `iced-juice`. Until that art exists the egg borrows `beach-drink`'s cell,
# which is a documented placeholder (a blue shard, not a drink).
#
# Rules learned from the other prop prompts in this folder:
#   - ONE object, dead centre, small in frame, generous flat margin around it.
#   - Straight top-down, as if seen from directly overhead. The game's beach
#     art is strictly plan view: no visible sides, no signature perspective.
#     A glass is mostly rim + liquid from above — that is the whole read.
#   - Flat single-colour background (a mid grey), no shadow on it, no props.
#   - Square, 512x512. The game draws it at `visual.size` px on a body that is
#     60 px wide, so fine interior detail is lost — silhouette first.
#
# --- prompt ---
#
# A single tall glass of iced juice, seen from DIRECTLY overhead, straight
# top-down orthographic view, no perspective, no visible side of the glass.
# Clear glass rim as a thin bright circle, filled with bright orange juice,
# two or three pale ice cubes floating on the surface, one thin straw leaning
# across the rim, a small green mint leaf and half a slice of orange resting on
# the rim as a garnish. Anime pixel-art style, clean 1-2 px outline, warm
# orange highlights, soft cel shading, palette matching a sunny tropical beach
# (#ff9d3d juice, #ffe6b8 ice, #3fa9a0 mint). The glass is centred, occupying
# about 60% of the frame, with a generous flat margin of plain mid-grey
# (#808080) around it. Flat neutral background, no cast shadow, no other
# objects, no text, no watermark.
#
# --- negative ---
#
# side view, three-quarter view, tilted camera, perspective, visible glass
# wall, table, hand, multiple glasses, splash, text, watermark, busy background
